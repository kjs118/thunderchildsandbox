About the Band

Rock. Loud.
Unfiltered.

Thunder Child is a high-energy rock cover band born in the heart of Acadiana. We bring the hits — from Deep Purple and Led Zeppelin to Soundgarden and Foo Fighters — played the way they were meant to be heard: cranked up and in your face.

Whether it's a packed bar, an outdoor festival, or a private event, we deliver the full experience every single night. No tracks. No shortcuts. Just five musicians who love rock and roll as much as you do.
