The Band

ABOUT

Who We
Are.

Thunder Child is a Lafayette, Louisiana-based rock cover band delivering high-energy performances across Acadiana and beyond.

We cover the full spectrum of rock — from the classic riffs of Led Zeppelin, Black Sabbath and Aerosmith to grunge and alt-rock of Pearl Jam, Alice in Chains, Queens of the Stone Age, and more.
